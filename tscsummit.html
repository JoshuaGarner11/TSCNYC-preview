TSC Summit.

A campus of Times Square Church on the grounds of Summit International School of Ministry in Grantville, PA, led by Senior Pastor Pavel Maftey.

What to expect.

Services run 1.5 to 2 hours, with Christ-centered worship and Biblically-sound teaching. Come as you are; there is no dress code.

After the service, stop by the Connect Table in the foyer for a free drink card at our coffee shop. On the first Sunday of every month, new guests are invited to a Connect Lunch with one of our pastors.

Kids and Sky Preschool.

TSC Kids is available Sunday mornings for children from birth through 5th grade, in a safe, Christ-centered environment.

Sky Preschool, a ministry of TSC Summit, offers a play-based, Christ-centered early childhood program surrounded by 100 acres of fields and nature.
