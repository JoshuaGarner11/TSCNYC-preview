A legacy restored.

On September 23, 1857, a businessman named Jeremiah Lanphier waited alone in a third-floor room in lower Manhattan for a noon prayer meeting. At 12:30, one man joined him.

Within six months, 10,000 people were gathering daily for prayer across New York City. It became known as the Fulton Street Prayer Revival.

Today, Times Square Church is reviving this hour of prayer, which is also part of our legacy as a church.

“In the heart of this city, in the middle of the week, we’re inviting you to pause. To pray. To encounter God.”

Pastor Tim Dilena
