A testimony ofobedience.

Times Square Church was founded by Pastor David Wilkerson in October 1987.

Called to New York.

Pastor David was first called to New York City in 1958 to minister to gang members and drug addicts, as told in The Cross and the Switchblade.

The book has sold over 50 million copies in over 30 languages. He went on to create Teen Challenge and World Challenge, Inc. to minister to people’s spiritual and physical needs.

42nd Street, 1986.

Walking down 42nd Street at midnight, Pastor David’s heart broke over what he saw.

Runaways, drug addicts and hustlers filled the streets. He cried out for God to do something, anything, to help the physically destitute and spiritually dead people he saw.

“I wept and prayed, ‘God, you’ve got to raise up a testimony in this hellish place.’ The answer was not what I wanted to hear: ‘Well, you know the city. You’ve been here. You do it.’”

David Wilkerson

From Town Hall to Broadway.

Pastor David obeyed God and opened Times Square Church in October 1987 in Town Hall, before moving to its present home at the Mark Hellinger Theater.

In 1994 he founded Summit International School of Ministry. His passion to disciple believers, build up leaders and care for the poor is still at the heart of Times Square Church today.
