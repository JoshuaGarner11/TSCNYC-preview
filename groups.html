Common questions.

How often do groups meet?

Most meet once a week through each quarter: winter, spring, summer and fall.

Is there childcare?

It varies. Each group’s profile says whether childcare is available.

What if the first group isn’t a fit?

Try a couple. We believe there is a group that is right for everyone.
